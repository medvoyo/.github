### What we do

We help international healthcare professionals get registered, pass the right exams and relocate, with a written route plan, document checks before you submit and an adviser at every step.

| | |
|---|---|
| 🩺 **Professions** | Doctors · Nurses and midwives · Dentists · Pharmacists · Physiotherapists · Radiographers · Biomedical scientists · Occupational therapists |
| 🌍 **Destinations** | United Kingdom · Ireland · Germany · United States |
| 🧭 **Registration routes** | GMC · NMC · HCPC · GDC · GPhC · IMC · NMBI · CORU · ECFMG · NCLEX · USMLE · Approbation |
| 🧮 **Free tools** | Cost calculator · Pathway checker · NHS take-home pay · Timeline planner · English test checker · Germany recognition finder · Visa Bulletin checker |

### How we work

1. **Free assessment**: tell us your degree, country, experience and goal
2. **Route plan**: the fastest route, exams, fees and a realistic timeline
3. **Exams and registration**: the right order, and your file checked before you submit
4. **Job, visa and move**: CV, interviews, sponsorship and family visas, timed to your start date

### Our standards

- Every fee and rule is taken from the regulator, exam body or government source, with the date we checked it
- Independent: not affiliated with the NHS, GMC, NMC, HCPC, ECFMG or any government body
- No invented statistics, testimonials or success rates
